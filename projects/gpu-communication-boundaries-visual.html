Two Platforms, One Boundary

The investigation asked what a socket boundary costs GPU-to-GPU traffic. Five labs on a four-V100 workstation and one paid session on four L40S answered it, and the answer turned out to be about mechanism, not placement. Every number here is read from a file under results/; the page is a picture of the evidence, not the evidence. Cross-platform comparisons are patterns, never a controlled experiment.

What NCCL actually chose, per platform and setting

The driver's P2P matrix says what is possible. NCCL's debug log says what it used. The two disagreed on both machines, and on the L40S the choice depended on how many GPUs were in the communicator.

Boundary or mechanism? Hold one, change the other

All-reduce bus bandwidth at 256 MB, median of the protocol runs. Matched-transport comparisons showed no measurable boundary penalty. Changing transport produced approximately 15 to 18 percent bandwidth differences. The V100 could never fill its fourth cell; the L40S filled it and the pattern held.

How the investigation got here

Six labs, in order. Each one changed what the next one asked.

  1. LAB-012026-08-23
    The instrument works. Protocol, controls, and the first V100 baseline (8.30 GB/s, 4 GPUs). Run-to-run spread under 1 percent at every size.V100
  2. LAB-022026-08-23
    The boundary looked expensive, then didn't. A 2 percent cross-socket deficit traced to CPU binding, not the socket. NCCL's default ran SHM on a pair whose P2P the driver allowed; PHB forced P2P and bought +16 percent.V100
  3. LAB-A12026-08-24
    A coin flip per launch. Small-message latency sits at one of two levels, chosen per process, machine-wide, about one launch in seven high. No gap dependence.V100
  4. LAB-032026-08-25
    Unbound, no boundary penalty was measured; the forced ring lost bandwidth. Cross-socket matched same-socket. Forcing PHB on the four-GPU ring cost 10 percent of bandwidth. A NUMA prefix on the ring cost 33 percent.V100
  5. LAB-042026-08-27
    The application disagrees with the benchmark. The +15 percent pair-bandwidth gain accompanied 3.2 percent lower training throughput. Four-micro-batch accumulation improved throughput by 12 percent, but also changed optimizer-update frequency. A thermal gate was born after the chassis hit 84 °C.V100
  6. LAB-052026-10-04
    Same question, second row of the table. NCCL's default used P2P for two GPUs and SHM for four. No measurable boundary penalty under matched transports; host-staging reduced pair bandwidth by 18 percent; the mixed ring loses 37 percent. 81 metered minutes.L40S
