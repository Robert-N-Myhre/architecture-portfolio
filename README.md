# Independent Technology Research

This repository publishes my independent technology investigations at:

**https://robert-n-myhre.github.io/architecture-portfolio/**

I explore questions about AI infrastructure, human–AI collaboration, distributed systems, networking, security, automation, and emerging technologies through prototypes, experiments, and architectural analysis.

Each investigation starts with a question that interested me, whether or not it led to a working system. My interests and conclusions can change as the evidence develops.

## Research and evidence

- **Experimental research:** measurement, controlled comparisons, and observed behavior.
- **Prototype-driven research:** working systems built to test architectural hypotheses.
- **Architectural research:** models and frameworks examined through reasoning and constraints.
- **Conceptual investigations:** proposed architectures not necessarily implemented or proven.

Projects explicitly identify what was proposed, observed, validated, or remains unknown. Negative results and stopped experiments are research outcomes, not omissions.
