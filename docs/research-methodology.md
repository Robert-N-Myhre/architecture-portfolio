# Research Methodology

This is a personal, independent research archive, not a claim that every idea has been proven or a repository of commercial deliverables.

## The starting point is a question

An investigation might begin with an unexpected observation, an architectural possibility, a technology I want to understand, or an assumption worth challenging. The question may evolve as I learn. I try to preserve that evolution rather than rewrite the story as though the outcome was obvious from the beginning.

## Different investigations need different methods

- **Experimental research** uses measurement and comparison to examine actual behavior.
- **Prototype-driven research** implements enough of a system to test a hypothesis or expose architectural constraints.
- **Architectural research** explores models, relationships, tradeoffs, and boundary conditions through explicit reasoning.
- **Conceptual investigations** develop possible systems without claiming that their designs have been implemented or validated.

These are useful descriptions, not mandatory document templates. A project can involve more than one method.

## Evidence and claims

I distinguish:

- **Question or hypothesis:** what I want to understand or suspect may be true.
- **Proposed design:** what I think might satisfy a set of constraints.
- **Observed result:** what a particular experiment or implementation actually showed.
- **Validated finding:** what is supported by defined tests and their limitations.
- **Open question:** what the current evidence does not settle.

Prototype results do not establish production suitability. A reference architecture can be useful while remaining unproven. A stalled or rejected approach is still worth documenting when the reasoning is informative.

## Publication boundaries

The work here is independently initiated. Employer- and client-directed architectures, organizational accomplishments, and confidential operational material are outside the scope of this site. Professional background may provide context, but is not the purpose of the archive.

## No required outcome

An investigation can remain conceptual, be deferred, change direction, or end in a negative result. This site is a record of questions I chose to explore and what I was able to learn—not a list of technologies I set out to endorse.
